Automotive Engineering & Industry 5.0

Maruti Suzuki india Limited operation one of the largest passanger car manufacturing ecosystems in Asia. The project highlights the core industrial,operations across key plant location in Gurugram,IMT Manesar ,and Hasalpur (Gujrat).

Key Plant Highlights & Production Stats

  • Annual Capacity:

    2 million Vehicles per Year

  • Plant Location:

    (Haryana) ,IMT Manesar (Haryana), Hansalpur (Gujrat), IMT Kharkhoda (Haryana)

  • Manufacturing Speed:

    1 Car Manufactured Every 12 seconds

  • Plant Operational Status:

    Functional & Optimized

Press & Stamping Shop

  • Function:

    Stamping raw sheet metal into structure vehicle panels.

  • Key Tech:

    high-speed hydraulic press lines.

  • Output:

    Car door, bonnets, roof panels, and structure chassis components.

Robotic Weld Shop

  • Function:

    Joining stamped metal parts together to build the car body shell(Body-in-white).

  • Key Tech:

    High-density robotic spot welding and automated inspection sensors.

  • Output:

    Completed structural body shell.

Paint Shop

  • Function:

    Multi-stage protective coating and automated body painting.

  • Key Tech:

    Cathodic Electro Deposition (CED) anti-rust coating and robotic spray arms.

  • Output:

    Corrosion-resistant,finished painted body.

Powertrain-& final Assembly Line

  • Function:

    Installing the engine,transmission,dashboard,glass,wheels and interior.

  • Key Tech:

    overhead conveyor belts and automated torque tools.

  • Output:

    assembeld vehicle ready for quality inspection.

Industrial Quality & Lean Principles Used

  • 5S System:

    Workplace organization (Sort,Set in order, Shine,Standarize,Sustain).

  • Kaizen:

    Continuous small operational improvements suggested by plant workers.

  • Poka-Yoka:

    Error-proofing techniques integrated into assembely line machines.

  • Shower & Tracking Testing:

    Every finished car undergoes water-leak tests and track performance checks before dispatch.
